## 14B (local, PySR) galaxy rotation curves - first physics step
Data: SPARC (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016), MassModels_Lelli2016c.mrt and SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt, downloaded 2026-10-07
(SHA-256 9108994b..., 5aa0501f...). Cuts as McGaugh et al. 2016: quality flag Q < 3, inclination >= 30 deg, dV/V < 0.1.
g_obs = V_obs^2 / R; g_bar = (V_gas|V_gas| + 0.5 V_disk^2 + 0.7 V_bul^2) / R. Split by GALAXY: 2/3 fit, 1/3 held out (seeded).
14B-1 (positive control): PySR (same settings as the Planck step) on log g_bar -> log g_obs must find a formula whose held-out
  RMS (dex) is within 10% of the published RAR g_obs = g_bar / (1 - exp(-sqrt(g_bar / g+))) with g+ fitted on the fit set;
  and g+ must come out within 20% of 1.2e-10 m/s^2.
14B-2 (exploratory): PySR on the RAR residuals using galaxy properties (central surface brightness, gas fraction, R / R_disk,
  Hubble type) as extra inputs. A "second-parameter law" is reported only if it lowers the held-out RMS by >= 5% with
  <= 2 extra constants AND the same search on galaxy-shuffled properties does not (control).
No claim beyond this data set; any law found needs its own registration and an independent data set.
