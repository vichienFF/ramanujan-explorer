## B6-1 (Kaggle, 1 kernel) q-PCF search, Rogers-Ramanujan style  [new method]
Family: x(q) = a_0(q) + b_1(q)/(a_1(q) + b_2(q)/(a_2(q) + ...)) with
  a_n(q) = alpha + beta q^n + gamma q^(2n),  alpha in {1,2}, beta, gamma in [-3,3];
  b_n(q) = delta q^(k n + t) (1 + eps q^n),  delta in {-2,-1,1,2}, k in {1,2,3}, t in {-1,0,1} with k + t >= 1, eps in [-2,2].
Evaluate as an exact integer power series to O(q^300) (forward recurrence, truncated). Normalise F = x / (leading term).
Product test: write F = prod_{m>=1} (1 - q^m)^(-c_m) (computed from log F); HIT if all c_m (m <= 300) are integers and
periodic with some period M <= 60 (at least 5 full periods), i.e. F is an eta/theta-type product (modular-type identity).
Also test x itself after dividing by q^(v) for the leading power, and the ratio x / x(q -> q^2) is NOT used (keep it simple).
Positive controls (must be found by the same code): Rogers-Ramanujan CF (a_n = 1, b_n = q^n; period 5), Ramanujan's cubic
CF (period 6), Gollnitz-Gordon CF (period 8). Negative control: 1,000 random integer series of the same length -> 0 hits.
Output: all hits with (M, exponent pattern). Novelty: hits are compared by hand with the known CFs listed above and with
Andrews-Berndt (Lost Notebook) and Slater's list; reported as "known" or "not found in the checked sources".
Success (exploratory): any hit not among the controls and not found in the checked sources.
