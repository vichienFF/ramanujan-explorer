# Amendment 22-5 to PLAN_BATCH22 (2026-10-14) - Route C step (a): do record multipliers have structure?

Labels: [VERIFIED] [SOURCE] [INFERENCE] [OPINION]. Chosen by the user (items 1 + 3 after Route B).
Context: Route B (Amendment 22-4) gave a VALID log-growth result for g*(q) of Bose sets, close to random sets. Route C (a):
if the multipliers t that give the largest gaps sit in special cosets (related to the subfield F_q, i.e. to q - 1 or q + 1),
a structural (provable) construction may exist; if they look uniform, the random model is the right lens.

Data: recompute, for every Bose set of Amendment 22-4 (primes q in [101, 2003]), the 10 multipliers with the largest gaps
(one per class {+-t, +-tq}); same for the 60 random-set null runs (multipliers without the q-class reduction).
Statistics (pooled over q): fractional residues u = (t mod (q+1))/(q+1) and v = (t mod (q-1))/(q-1); also
w = log(ord_m(t))/log(m) (relative multiplicative order).
Reference distribution: the same statistics for ALL admissible multipliers of the same q (exact, not sampled) - the
question is whether record multipliers differ from typical multipliers.
Test: two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (record vs all) for u, v, w; significance after Bonferroni (3 tests) at 0.01.
Control: the identical procedure on the null random sets must NOT reject (all three p > 0.01); otherwise the test is VOID.
Outcome: STRUCTURE FOUND (which statistic, effect size) -> describe and propose a construction; NO STRUCTURE -> the random
model stays the working hypothesis for Route C. Local run (minutes).
